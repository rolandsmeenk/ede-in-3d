# Ede church exterior models

The four local models use their individual 3DBAG LoD 2.2 church shells and ground footprints. Coordinates are in the Oude Kerk scene grid, with vertical levels in metres above NAP before export to scene Y. The native 0.5 m PDOK AHN DTM samples are from exposed ground within 1.2 m of each footprint. AHN DSM readings give the top of the visible roof or tower, including the fine spires missed by LoD 2.2. The models share the tileable masonry material and have one indexed footprint collider each. [3DBAG](https://docs.3dbag.nl/en/) · [PDOK AHN coverage service](https://service.pdok.nl/rws/ahn/wcs/v1_0?service=WCS&request=GetCapabilities)

Exterior photographs served as offline measurement and colour references. The models ship as local geometry with reusable procedural materials.

Chrome WebGPU verification used both a street-level and aerial view of each landmark. Walking-controller replays reached all four entry approaches within 0.55 m of the planned endpoint. Overhead rays through the surveyed church interiors hit the custom roofs and returned zero visible fallback or streamed 3DBAG hits.
