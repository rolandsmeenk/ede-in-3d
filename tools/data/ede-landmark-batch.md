# De Keetmolen, De Peppel, CHE, churches and Lukkien

The eight destinations use local geometry and materials. The landmark registry replaces the matching 3DBAG and fallback features by their mapped IDs. Neighboring parcels stay in the normal building stream. All heights below use NAP or the existing Oude Kerk scene datum, which is 22.54 m NAP.

## In-game verification

- The De Keetmolen mound was walked in both directions. The controlled climb reached a scene height of about 2.02 m. The sail tip is visible above the trees from the Ede-Wageningen forecourt. Nearby trees and buildings screen much of the tower from the station. The Stationsweg and aerial views show the full silhouette.
- Both De Peppel entrances were reached on foot. The pool and hall were checked separately from the street and together from above.
- The CHE campus and sports hall were viewed from their approaches and above. All ten walking and cycling checks passed. These cover both directions beneath the skybridge and both directions on each building approach. Measured in-game clearance was 4.33–4.41 m. The bridge ray found the modeled roof and underside without an overlapping generic roof.
- All four church walking approaches passed. Overhead rays found the custom roofs and no duplicate visible fallback or 3DBAG geometry. [Church verification](ede-church-landmarks.md)
- The Lukkien teleport lands on Copernicuslaan facing the ground-level entrance. The in-game collision probe reached the glazed doors from the road with no lateral push. The deck at scene position `(-3258.95, 4.24, 2622.56)` is 26.78 m NAP. In the running WebGPU game, Q brought the helicopter down from 12 m above the deck. It stopped on the surface with zero vertical velocity. Space then lifted it 10.03 m from the settled position. A downward ray hit the deck mesh at the registered elevation.
