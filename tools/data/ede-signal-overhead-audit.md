# Overhead traffic lights in Ede

The audit compared 334 mapped signal-control points and the municipal VRI inventory with paired 2025 and 2026 PDOK aerial imagery. These form 54 junction and crossing groups in the playable area. The reviewed layout records feed the offline signal bake. [Reviewed placements](ede-reviewed-overhead-signals.json) · [Signal sources and bake](ede-signal-hardware.sources.md)

The aerial photos show physical supports and heads. The municipal register identifies installations, and the directed OSM controls establish the approaches. The renderer checks the support feet against same-level motor and cycle paving. Aerial placements use estimated mounting heights and standard circular lenses.

The updated bake adds 201 overhead heads. An offline Chrome check found 192 at their intended positions and facing their approaches. The renderer holds nine in review at the two Verlengde Arnhemseweg junctions and eastbound Lorentzstraat because the current road strips cover their supports.

At the hospital, an unfiltered 300 m query returned zero rows from 1GIS furniture layers 13 and 31 and the frozen DTB point and line layers. Frozen KernGIS Droog returned zero furniture points and zero gantry or support lines in the junction window. Gemeente Ede lists the location as VRI 2. [Legacy DTB](https://geo.rijkswaterstaat.nl/arcgis/rest/services/GDR/dtb/MapServer) · [Legacy KernGIS Droog](https://geo.rijkswaterstaat.nl/arcgis/rest/services/GDR/kerngis_droog/MapServer) · [Municipal VRI 2](https://geo.ede.nl/geoserver/wfs?service=WFS&version=2.0.0&request=GetFeature&typeNames=Ede%3Amor_verkeersinstallaties&featureID=mor_verkeersinstallaties.2&outputFormat=application%2Fjson)

The lane fits at VRI 2 use the full same-level road surfaces and retain the narrower upstream sections. Northbound Dr. W. Dreeslaan goes from two lanes to five and reaches 15.3 m of modeled lane width at the stop line. The other three approaches reach 15.8 m, 16.2 m with the separate westbound bus lane, and 12.6 m. The rebuilt bus asset retains the same trips and matched stops.

The stop bars at VRI 5 and seven further approaches use measurements from the linked 2025 and 2026 photos. The road graph and paint share those positions. The four VRI 25 approaches use separately traced lane dividers, including the Akulaan bus lane. VRI 4 uses the two Keesomstraat approaches. VRI 29 uses the west approach. The west Galvanistraat bar lies at an existing bend used by the bus graph, and the other VRI 29 approaches have upstream changes in lane count that need separate geometry. [Measured lane and bar coordinates](../../js/ede-surveyed-approaches.js)

At Bennekomseweg / Enkalaan, VRI 65 has a visible cantilever and lacks a mapped local traffic control. Westbound Lorentzstraat has a surveyed arm whose footing conflicts with the rendered road strip. Several spans and heads remain in review where a tree, hard shadow, or older image obscures the current layout.
