# Five school exteriors

The scene geometry comes from classified LoD 2.2 wall and roof surfaces. The offline bake uses the pinned RDNAPTRANS2018 transformation and the Oude Kerk affine. All heights are relative to the scene origin of 22.54 m NAP. The site records list the surveyed building IDs and the matching OpenStreetMap footprints. The approach points are on the visible entrance forecourts. Kadaster’s 2025 tile uses 2025 AHN6 at the Dulon and Lyceum locations. It captures Dulon after renovation and contains the 2024 Lyceum, whose ID has no 3DBAG CityJSON feature. Façade fittings are model geometry and procedural lettering, traced from published views. The reference photographs are used for measurement and are outside the runtime assets.
