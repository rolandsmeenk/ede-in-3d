# Restaurant exteriors

Eight measured 3DBAG LoD 2.2 building shells use source IDs for precise replacement. Villa Brutus adds geometry outside the existing walls at Museumplein. The models use procedural surfaces, with photos as survey references. 3DBAG © tudelft3d and 3DGI, CC BY 4.0. [3DBAG attribution](https://docs.3dbag.nl/en/copyright/) · [OpenStreetMap attribution](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright)

| Site | Survey and exterior references |
| --- | --- |
| Het Pomphuis | BAG `0228100000068985`. Twin white gables with the `GW` and `1915` tablets, rounded bay, front arch, awnings and garden terrace. [Historisch Museum Ede](https://www.collectiegelderland.nl/historischmuseumede/object/2e520e7f-92d8-c9ff-9217-844e7b317872) · [Allard Bijlsma's 2021 and 2026 photos](https://www.atlasvanede.nl/ede-toen/bedrijven-toen/nutsbedrijven/pomphuis) · [Terrace photos](https://www.klaassenbv.nl/app/uploads/sure/media/33811701/33811701-58990-189836_a2a102e8-a84f-4adf-8ada-db1dac8645ad.pdf) |
| Stationsweg 2–14 | BAG `0228100000003228`, `0228100000042576` and `0228100000052503`. Former Hotel GEA with wraparound balcony, Da Aldo's bay window and striped awnings, and Bistro Twaalf 14's blue frontage. [Allard Bijlsma's photo survey and building history](https://www.atlasvanede.nl/mooi-ede/must-see/21-30-in-ede/21-statige-huizen-aan-de-stationsweg/stationsweg-even-huis-voor-huis) |
| Boerderij Hoekelum · De Koffiekamer | BAG `0228100000043795`, `0228100000016500` and `0228100000014390`. The café gable, farm wings and southeast terrace extend the separate castle and coach-house models. [Farm history](https://www.hoekelum.nl/historie/) · [Courtyard photograph](https://www.wandelzoekpagina.nl/wandeling/kort-en-prachtig-landgoed-hoekelum/21566/) |
| Pannenkoekenhuis De Langenberg | BAG `0228100000016671`. Skylights, mullioned glass terrace canopy, entrance and seating on the road side. [Operator photographs](https://pannenkoekenhuizen.com/de-langenberg/) · [Exterior view](https://onzeveluwe.nl/pannenkoekenhuis-langenberg) |
| Villa Brutus | Geometry for the conservatory, timber pergola and seating. BAG `0228100000058648` and `0228100000068490` retain the existing walls. [Operator exterior gallery](https://www.villabrutus.nl/foto-s) |

Roof footprints and terrace positions were checked against [PDOK aerial imagery](https://service.pdok.nl/hwh/luchtfotorgb/wms/v1_0). Some opening sizes, rear elevations and furniture placement are estimates from the available views.
