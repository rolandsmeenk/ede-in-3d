# NIZO, Peti Protein and the Loevestein skyline

- **Source geometry.** The existing buildings use 3DBAG LoD 2.2 shells under CC BY 4.0. The models use the game's Oude Kerk coordinate frame and AHN vertical datum. Matching OpenStreetMap outlines supply map access and the fallback building replacements. [3DBAG attribution](https://docs.3dbag.nl/en/copyright/)
- **NIZO Food Research, Kernhemseweg.** The north production block is BAG `0228100000018156` and OSM `w271586840`. Its surveyed stepped hall and tower measure 27.42 m from the surveyed ground. The five existing building replacements and Kernhemseweg arrival also serve the enlarged campus. [3DBAG record](https://api.3dbag.nl/collections/pand/items/NL.IMBAG.Pand.0228100000018156)
- **Peti Protein, Veredelingstraat 2, De Klomp.** BAG `0228100000085782` and OSM `w881326370`. The 26.21 m shell follows the survey. The frontage glazing, timber trim, grey cladding and concrete skirting follow Peti Protein's exterior photographs. The approach is on Veredelingstraat. [3DBAG record](https://api.3dbag.nl/collections/pand/items/NL.IMBAG.Pand.0228100000085782) · [Peti Protein](https://petiprotein.nl/over-peti-protein/)
- **Victoria Vesta, former offices.** BAG `0228100000008709` and `0228100000068581`, OSM `w1074318792` and `w1074318791`. The brown T-wing with its lighter added top floor and the buff corner block with dark red window frames follow KUDO's completed phases 1 and 2. The approach is on Victoriastein. [KUDO](https://www.kudo.nl/projecten/loevestein)
- **Victoria Veste, Proosdijpark.** BAG `0228100000088327` and OSM `w1348529488`. The registered footprint includes the underground garage. The western rectangle is the above-grade tower. The 2022 height survey predates the built tower. The 30 m roof and stepped top follow IBT's construction measurements, Hoekstra's completed floor plans and KUDO's completion photos. The approach is on the Kastelenlaan cycleway next to Proosdijpark. [BAG record](https://api.pdok.nl/kadaster/bag/ogc/v2/collections/pand/items/53970a15-81da-5d54-ac8f-b37cc4fa0337) · [IBT](https://bouwtechniek.nl/category/woningbouw/) · [Hoekstra](https://www.makelaardijhoekstra.nl/brochure-victoria-veste-ede/) · [KUDO](https://www.kudo.nl/projecten/loevestein-4-victoria-veste)
