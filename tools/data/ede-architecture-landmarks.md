# Six architectural landmarks

The six destinations use local 3DBAG LoD 2.2 shells and site-specific exterior geometry. OpenStreetMap way IDs and BAG building IDs identify the exact shells they replace. The ground-level street and path network stays in use. All six sites have map search entries and framed ground-level arrivals.

| Site | Model and references |
| --- | --- |
| Oranjestaete | Fitted terraced shell, angled smoked-glass railings, sloping concrete partitions and dark penthouse facing Oude Bennekomseweg. [Exterior photos and bake](ede-villa-heritage-landmarks.md) |
| Winkelcentrum Bellestein | The open-air shopping street and raised residential level use surveyed building volumes. The exterior includes shopfronts, covered passages and the documented upper walkway connections. [RCE shopping centre survey](https://www.cultureelerfgoed.nl/site/binaries/site-content/collections/documents/2026/01/01/post-65-winkelcentra-ontwikkeling-en-voorbeelden-1965-1990/post-65-winkelcentra-rce.pdf#page=33) · [Post’65 photos and drawings](https://post65.nl/winkelcentrum-bellestein-ede/) |
| P.L. Bergansiuskazerne | The U-shaped barracks has the main entrances, clock, roof overhangs, dormers and the visible details of the residential conversion. BAG `0228100000011008`, OSM `w271573812`. [Monument register](https://www.monumenten.nl/monument/523485) · [Atlas van Ede renovation photos](https://www.atlasvanede.nl/mooi-ede/gebouwen/appartementen/2021-heden/bergansiuskazerne) |
| ENKA EHBO-gebouw | The rounded entrance and glass bays follow the measured shell. The fountain and memorial bench use their restored positions across the square. BAG `0228100000080808`, OSM `w486521869`. [Monument register](https://www.monumenten.nl/monument/527069) · [BOEi relocation](https://www.boei.nl/projecten/enka-monumenten/) · [Restoration photos](https://enkahistorie.nl/bank-en-fontein/) |
| Terrasflat Laathof | Surveyed terraced volumes with planted balcony edges and concrete gallery lines on the park-facing side. [Post’65 photos and drawings](https://post65.nl/terrasflat-laathof-ede/) |
| Villa Sterrenberg | The surveyed hillside shell and annex carry the five-bay front, dressed window surrounds, central door and exterior steps. [Exterior photos and bake](ede-villa-heritage-landmarks.md) |

Façade fittings, terrace rails and exterior prop details use approximate dimensions derived from the references. Photos are survey references.
