# Ede parking demand sources

The parking model uses Europe/Amsterdam time. It interpolates occupation between survey anchors for the relevant area and uses time-of-day assumptions where the surveys provide no equivalent observation. Space-level placement and dwell are simulated. Survey percentages describe specific count dates and locations.

An exact facility reading is accepted only for the corresponding facility ID with a valid timestamp, occupied count and source capacity. The live adapter and the browser both limit freshness to three minutes from the source timestamp. After expiration, the facility summary resumes its survey or inferred designation and the displayed occupancy transitions toward the profile over 15 minutes. A reading gives a facility-wide count. The visible choice of spaces remains simulated.
