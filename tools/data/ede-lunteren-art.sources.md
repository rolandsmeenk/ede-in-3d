# Lunteren public art and memorials

These procedural models use local terrain elevations and the existing civic mesh batching. Reference photographs and the 2025 PDOK orthophoto were used for fitting geometry and positions. The scenery uses modeled materials and generated lettering. Plaque sizes, local rotations, and positions without surveyed coordinates are estimates.

- **Di tengah ombak** by Jaïr Pattipeilohy and Maurice Den Boer has a bronze outrigger canoe, a paddler, choppy waves and a dark natural-stone plinth. The municipality’s permit places it near Dorpsstraat 84. The model uses the title on the plinth. The available photographs did not yield a complete, legible record of the engraved family names. [Artist’s project](https://mauricedenboer.nl/moluks-monument-lunteren/), [Unveiling by Stichting Ana Upu](https://ana-upu.nl/geschiedenis/moluks-monument/), [Municipal permit 2017W2962](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/gmb-2018-20143.pdf)
- **Lunters Vrouwtje** by Elly van den Broek has a bronze dress, head covering and church book on an exposed-aggregate pedestal. The figure sits at the OpenStreetMap point. The corten-steel arrow and separate viewer stand at the southern tip of the Nieuwe Erf lawn and the arrow points toward the surveyed Middelpunt. [Local history](https://lunteren.nl/Bezoekers/Wist-je-dat), [Museum photograph](https://www.collectiegelderland.nl/historischmuseumede/object/9a3a3620-bea6-2abf-3d30-105f4656ea97), [OpenStreetMap figure](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/8482217795), [Goudsberg project’s photo and description](https://middelpuntvannederland.nl/afstand-middelpunt-van-nederland-nieuwe-erf-2462-meter/)
- **De Bron and De Meander** are by Floor van Dusseldorp. Four low stainless-steel waves occupy the station verge and follow the published 35 cm by 5 m by 2.5 m dimensions. A fitted trace of inlaid markers follows Wilbrinkstraat and joins the blue granito ribbon across Dorpsstraat, whose visible curve is traced from PDOK imagery. The individual route markers are representative placements along the photographed route. [Spoorbeeld art record](https://www.spoorbeeld.nl/databank/de-bron), [Local history of the brook](https://www.lunteren.com/nieuws/lunterse-beschouwinkjes-de-lunterse-beek/), [OpenStreetMap De Bron](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/6647232564), [OpenStreetMap De Meander](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/6647232563)
- **Centraal Monument and 75 jaar bevrijding** use the arched wooden back, brick wall and 50 cm square stone, plus a separate relief tile on a slanted weathering-steel pedestal near Roskammersteeg. The inscription on the installed bench plaque names the 5 Canadian Armoured Division and 16 April 1945. The committee’s transcription on the monument page instead names the 1 Canadian Infantry Division and 17 April 1945. Geometry and lettering follow the photographs of the physical monuments. [Nationaal Comité 4 en 5 mei record](https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/1292/lunteren-centraal-monument), [Installed plaque photograph](https://www.4en5mei.nl/app/uploads/bis-images/6110/487442_lunteren_centraal_monument_-3840x1060.jpg), [2025 local history feature](https://www.lunteren.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/wk-16.pdf), [75-year tile unveiling](https://ede.nieuws.nl/nieuws/gedenktegel-onthuld-van-75-jaar-bevrijding-van-lunteren)
- **Kerkhoflaan memorial and De Keuenklep** use the cemetery’s upright engraved boulder on rough stone steps and the Immenweg memorial boulder near a green wooden bench. The camera coordinates of the geotagged Immenweg photograph and the corresponding stone visible in PDOK place the latter beside the woodland track. The monument committee pins both the rock and bench over a kilometer farther northwest, in heath without a nearby path. Fine engravings use the photographed headings and short excerpts. [Kerkhoflaan monument record](https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/1300/lunteren-gedenksteen-aan-de-kerkhoflaan), [Cemetery entrance photograph](https://www.online-begraafplaatsen.nl/begraafplaats/369/Gemeentelijke-Begraafplaats-Lunteren), [Geotagged Immenweg photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Oorlogsmonument_wapendropping_Immenweg_-_Lunteren.jpg), [Stone record](https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/4350/lunteren-herinneringsmonument-bij-de-keuenklep), [Bench record](https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/4221/ede-gedenkbank-wapendropping-de-keuenklep)
- **Tegelroute** includes two pieces on the Nieuwe Erf gallery, *Landleven* by Coos Hoving and *Hugo de Vries* by Bep van Engen. The reliefs borrow the references’ color blocking and motifs, with estimated positions along the mapped gallery façade. [Tegelroute index](https://www.lunteren.com/cultureel/tegelroute/), [Landleven](https://www.lunteren.com/cultureel/tegelroute/landleven/), [Hugo de Vries](https://www.lunteren.com/cultureel/tegelroute/hugo-de-vries/)
