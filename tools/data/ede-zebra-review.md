The initial comparison found no published crossing that carried both VOP evidence and an explicit non-zebra OSM marking. It also compared the VOP carriageway polygons with published non-zebra OSM segments on the same level. A possible conflict required separation of at most 2.4 m and an absolute carriageway tangent dot product above 0.74. No segments met both conditions. A second scan of the local Gelderland and Utrecht OSM PBF extracts found 189 nodes in the playable bounds with explicit non-zebra markings. None were within 2.4 m of the 68 same-level VOP footprints. The review required no imagery overrides.

The browser rendered 187 crossings with known markings. The snapshot has 119 zebras, 37 dashed crossings and 31 dotted crossings. Paint widths and spacing use modelled dimensions where OSM records only the style.
