# Ede memorials

The 18 new sites are searchable by name on the map. They use local geometry and procedural inscriptions. Four new plaques and the Mausoleum lettering are details on existing landmarks. The Otterlo, Wekerom and De Wormshoef sites use the northern map coverage.

| Site | Evidence and placement |
| --- | --- |
| Joods Monument Ede | Bronze relief on a granite block on Vossenakker, placed from the map pin and PDOK imagery. [Nationaal Comité 4 en 5 mei](https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/4248/ede-joods-monument) · [Stichting Joods Monument Ede](https://stichtingjoodsmonumentede.nl/) |
| Indiëmonument en Stilteplaats | The memorial and the newer quiet area are grouped at the same map destination. Seating positions are fitted. [Gemeentearchief Ede](https://gemeentearchief.ede.nl/geschiedenis/indisch-monument-ede) |
| Monument Militaire Slachtoffers | Paasberg column and lettering from the monument register and photographs. The site follows the mapped south approach. [Nationaal Comité 4 en 5 mei](https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/1308/ede-gedenkzuil-op-de-paasberg) · [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/9819839610) |
| Canadees Bevrijdingsmonument | Inscribed boulder on Arnhemseweg. [Nationaal Comité 4 en 5 mei](https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/1307/ede-monument-voor-canadese-militairen) · [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/8248010314) |
| Grafmonument Anna Maria Moens | The surveyed location and photographs guide the cemetery monument. [Rijksmonumentenregister](https://monumentenregister.cultureelerfgoed.nl/monumenten/14465) · [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/665482600) |
| Canadese Gedenksteen Memorial Park | The small granite marker next to the Mausoleum. Its offset is fitted to the aerial and ground photos. [Ground photo](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mausoleum_Ede_foto_3.jpg) · [PDOK imagery](https://app.pdok.nl/viewer/) |

| De Amazones van Ede | Three figures on individual plinths fitted to the PDOK aerial. [Nationaal Comité 4 en 5 mei](https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/4943/ede-de-amazones-van-ede) · [Vereniging Oud Ede](https://www.oudede.nl/de-amazones-van-ede/) |

[Nationaal Comité 4 en 5 mei](https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/1302/ede-maple-leafs-polar-bearsmonument) · [Platform Militaire Historie Ede](https://www.platformmhe.nl/monumenten.php) |

| Gedenksteen bij de Oude Kerk | Grey stone with relief lettering, fitted low on the tower wall near the Grotestraat approach. The exact offset is approximate. [Nationaal Comité 4 en 5 mei](https://www.4en5mei.nl/oorlogsmonumenten/zoeken/1304/ede-gedenksteen-bij-de-oude-kerk) |
