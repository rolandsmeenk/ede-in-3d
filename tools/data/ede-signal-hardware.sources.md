# Ede signal hardware sources and refresh

The offline signal bake uses the existing OSM control positions and way identifiers from the base map and corridor overlay. OpenStreetMap contributors supply these references under ODbL 1.0. The bake fits physical hardware to nearby directed motor approaches and leaves the control positions, traffic graph and simulated cycle independent of the hardware inventory. BGT and 1GIS survey inputs are CC0 1.0. [OpenStreetMap attribution and licence](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright)

[PDOK BGT pole collection](https://api.pdok.nl/lv/bgt/ogc/v1/collections/paal?f=html) · [Rijkswaterstaat 1GIS heads](https://geo.rijkswaterstaat.nl/arcgis/rest/services/GDR/geografische_areaalregistratie/MapServer/13) · [Rijkswaterstaat 1GIS supports](https://geo.rijkswaterstaat.nl/arcgis/rest/services/GDR/geografische_areaalregistratie/MapServer/31)

The asset supplies 55 overhead heads and 17 roadside heads at 33 OSM controls. Four roadside heads use measured 1GIS records and 13 use surveyed BGT pole positions with a fitted head height. The overhead heads match eight gantries and twelve cantilever masts. A portal's survey Z may describe the base or a point on the span. Where the span Z agrees with the heads, it is retained. Other newly modeled span heights use the median head elevation plus a 0.56 m mounting offset and carry an estimate flag. Reused spans retain their existing beam height. Head point elevations retain their NAP evidence, with road-relative clearances checked against the matching fitted level and the applicable AHN ground grid.

Before placement, the bake withheld 84 heads for uncertain approach or mounting, 18 for uncertain support feet, three for a roadway conflict, and three for invalid elevation. It merged 21 duplicate points and one overlapping roadside face. The Ede municipal installation at the hospital identifies the junction. The published point layers did not supply its support alignment, so the 2025 photographs provide a separate survey lead. Exact backplate and visor shapes need a photograph or an as-built drawing for individual sites. [Ede cycling and signals project](https://www.ede.nl/in-de-gemeente-ede/ede-fietst/fietsvriendelijke-verkeerslichten) · [Hospital junction photos](https://www.atlasvanede.nl/thema-s/infrastructuur/snelfietsroutes/flip-stoplichten) · [CROW signal layouts](https://kennisbank.crow.nl/public/gastgebruiker/VMGT/Handboek_verkeerslichtenregelingen_2022/De_plaats_van_de_elementen_van_de_verkeersregelinstallatie/114342)

The standard three-aspect backplates follow the semicircular ends in TNO R11704 figure 1 and the manufacturer's photographed shield. The 200 mm lens version uses a 600 × 1,190 mm plate with a 30 mm white border. The 300 mm version uses a 900 × 1,600 mm plate with a 50 mm border. The face is black and the rear is pebble gray, following the Rijkswaterstaat specification. The survey asset supplies positions and heights, so lens size and exact manufacturer are inferred at each site. The rendered white border uses a matte material. The model seats the shield at the front cover, with separate openings for the projecting visors and lenses. This follows the pictured three-aspect assembly and the supplier’s cover-mounted bracket specification. [TNO figure 1](https://publications.tno.nl/publication/34619865/CbM779/TNO-2015-R11704.pdf#page=6) · [Municipality of Oss dimensions](https://www.oss.nl/to2/VRI-nieuw-te-plaatsen-installaties.htm) · [Hoeflake product photo](https://verkeerslantaarn.nl/informatie/onderdelen/achtergrondschilden/) · [TrafficSupply cover mounting specification and installed photo](https://www.informatiebord.nl/p/1038/verkeerslichten/achtergrondschilden/achtergrondschild-3-lichts-verkeerslicht-vkl/) · [Rijkswaterstaat specification, TE-103](https://www.tenderned.nl/papi/tenderned-rs-tns/v2/publicaties/213587/documenten/6358600/content#page=28)

The renderer fits the complete signal footprint to the corresponding BGT level, including polygon holes. The existing OSM road and cycle strips cover gaps. The runtime keeps standard backplates where the housing clears paving and uses slim visored heads on constrained mounts. On the A30 near Ede, the reused cantilever `1gis:31:2102709001` now stands at X −3966.42, Z 1319.11, the clear end of the surveyed span. Site-specific photos are still needed for the spans with unresolved feet, including two near Rondweg-Oost.

The Dutch placement rule measures the clearance from the pavement to the shield’s lower edge, or the housing’s lower edge on a slim head. It gives ordinary roadside heads a range of 2.20–2.40 m and overhead heads a minimum of 4.50 m inside the built-up area or 5.00 m outside. The game targets 2.30 m for inferred or out-of-range ordinary roadside mounts and checks overhead heads using 5.00 m as the map-wide minimum. Four checked roadside heads previously measured 1.71–2.98 m above the modeled pavement. All four now measure 2.30 m. [Regeling verkeerslichten, points 17–18](https://kennisbank.crow.nl/public/gastgebruiker/VMGT/Handboek_verkeerslichtenregelingen_2022/Bijlage_I_%C2%AD_Regeling_verkeerslichten/114590)

The 8 October 2022 photo of the west A30 exit at Schutterweg shows both shields centered vertically on the cantilever beam. The 1GIS beam line is at 15.598 m NAP and the two lamp points are at 16.105 and 16.161 m NAP. The photo establishes the mounting alignment, while the baked asset retains both original lamp elevations. The cantilever uses a cylindrical beam at that surveyed height. With the heads centered, their lower edges clear the modeled pavement by about 5.42 m and the beam’s modeled underside clears it by about 6.05 m. [2022 site photo by AS Media](https://112pressroom.nl/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/20221008143848.jpg) · [Rijkswaterstaat 1GIS support records](https://geo.rijkswaterstaat.nl/arcgis/rest/services/GDR/geografische_areaalregistratie/MapServer/31)

The same Schutterweg photo shows left arrows on both heads, recorded as `arrow: "left"` for 1GIS features `237719` and `496247`. The renderer supports all five Dutch directions across red, amber and green. A reviewed installation can also specify a separate `greenArrow` with a `direction`, mounting `side` as seen by the approaching driver, and the main-head `phases` during which it lights. No auxiliary green arrow has site evidence in this snapshot. Other heads use circular lenses until their optics are verified. The public 1GIS head layer supplies positions, asset labels and drawing references. Its schema has no lane ID or lens symbol. [Dutch signal specification, points 22–26](https://kennisbank.crow.nl/public/gastgebruiker/VMGT/Handboek_verkeerslichtenregelingen_2022/Bijlage_I_%C2%AD_Regeling_verkeerslichten/114590) · [Schutterweg reference photo](https://112pressroom.nl/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/20221008143848.jpg) · [1GIS head layer schema](https://geo.rijkswaterstaat.nl/arcgis/rest/services/GDR/geografische_areaalregistratie/MapServer/13)

The expanded map rendered 47 surveyed overhead heads, 17 surveyed roadside heads, 260 generated motor heads and 276 generated pedestrian heads in the November 2026 browser check. The game moved 442 post candidates and omitted 87 post candidates and three supports when no clear footing was available. A footprint check of 569 instanced posts and new gantry feet found zero same-level overlaps with mapped motor or cycle pavement. Seven shared motorway supports reuse checked feet in the existing infrastructure layer. Road control positions remain those of the source graph throughout.

Fetch the four source responses and bake the local asset. Reuse the snapshot directory and omit `--fetch` to rebuild offline. The inputs use the checked-in base map, corridor overlay, AHN grids, grade profiles and 1GIS infrastructure asset.

```sh
uv run --python 3.12 --with pyproj==3.8.0 --with 'shapely>=2.1' \
  python -B tools/build-ede-signal-hardware.py /tmp/ede-signal-hardware-20261121 --date 2026-11-21 --fetch
```
