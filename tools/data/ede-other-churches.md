# Additional church exteriors in Ede

The building bake uses 3DBAG LoD 2.2 for footprints, roof planes, walls and base levels. OSM identifies the corresponding fallback building. Geometry is exported into the Oude Kerk scene frame and retains NAP heights for placement. Exterior photographs inform colours, window rhythms, entrances, bell openings and details too fine for the survey. Their façade dimensions and trim colours are approximate. Photographs serve as reference for modelled details and shared materials.
