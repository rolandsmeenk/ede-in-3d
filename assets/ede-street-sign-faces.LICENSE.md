# Ede street-sign face artwork

The atlas includes SVGs from the Nationaal Dataportaal Wegverkeer QGIS road-sign catalogue at revision `24d3c1aa0e3dc14ff50cb58916efd82db4cbbd31`. The builder rasterizes the artwork, adds recorded sign values, mirrors matched arrow variants and composes zone panels as needed. [NDW source catalogue](https://github.com/ndwnu/qgis-verkeersborden-style/tree/24d3c1aa0e3dc14ff50cb58916efd82db4cbbd31)

The E8c symbol comes from the published Dutch statutory sign model. [Staatsblad 2022, 413](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2022-413.html)
