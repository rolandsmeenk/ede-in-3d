# Ede façade evidence

Façade layout and material measurements use reviewed Mapillary views tied to stable BAG building and wall identifiers. The image IDs and capture information accompany the offline review records.

- [Oude Kerk west entrance and tower window](https://www.mapillary.com/app/?pKey=1150151592642586) by [ligfietser](https://www.mapillary.com/app/user/ligfietser), 27 February 2024.
- [Torenstraat storefront](https://www.mapillary.com/app/?pKey=1420589411891656) by [ligfietser](https://www.mapillary.com/app/user/ligfietser), 27 February 2024.

Residential reviews around Kolkakkerweg use images by [thewizard](https://www.mapillary.com/app/user/thewizard). The [reviewed measurements](../tools/data/ede-facade-observations.json) list the other source image IDs, dates and creators.
