# Ede façade photo atlas

The image `ede-facade-pilot.webp` contains rectified, cropped, and exposure-adjusted adaptations of the following Mapillary photos by [ligfietser](https://www.mapillary.com/app/user/ligfietser). Both images were captured on 27 February 2024.

- [Oude Kerk west entrance and window](https://www.mapillary.com/app/?pKey=1150151592642586)
- [Torenstraat storefront](https://www.mapillary.com/app/?pKey=1420589411891656)
